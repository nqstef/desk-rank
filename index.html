    The ladder

      Reminders 5 min before

      Get an alert on your lock screen before every block. They come through the iPhone Calendar app, so they arrive even when Desk Rank is closed.
      Turn on reminders
      1. Tap the button, then Subscribe.
      2. Leave Remove Alerts switched off.
      3. Allow Calendar notifications if asked.

      Rules

      • A block unlocks at its start time. You cannot tick the future.
      • Yesterday stays open until 12:00 today, then it locks for good.
      • Finish 70% of a day's XP to hold your streak. Each streak day adds 5% to everything you earn, up to x1.50.
      • A perfect day pays a 50 XP bonus.
      • Under 70% breaks the streak. Under 40% also costs 75 XP, and you can be demoted.
      • Free time is free. It carries no XP and never counts against you.

      Backup This phone only

      Progress is stored on this phone. Copy a backup code now and then and keep it in Notes.
